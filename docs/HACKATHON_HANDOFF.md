## Submission description reference

Tour Rescue helps a touring artist respond to a cancelled concert without rebuilding the whole tour. Qloo provides cultural-fit evidence over a fixed city universe; a deterministic checker keeps confirmed dates, directed road evidence and declared constraints intact. The app compares a replacement, a detour and a day off, shows why a small assumption change can reverse the recommendation, and exports a source-bearing audit and promoter draft.

Optional Sonnet research uses tools for the current Qloo context, route evidence, public research, deterministic validation and private band knowledge. The user explicitly accepts a proposal. Missing venue availability, fees and terms remain UNKNOWN. Editable local band notes and previous accepted decisions are retrieved for the task and included in the audit when read. No venue is contacted or booked.

The mobile-first PWA also has a full desktop workspace, six-step keyboard-accessible guide, evidence controls and A4/PNG/JSON exports. Its offline synthetic case is available without a provider key in the browser; live actions run through the existing Worker with bounded usage.
