# Tour Rescue — final-v4 jury film

Two minutes at 1920×1080/60 fps: [MP4](TourRescue-jury-final-v4.mp4), [English VTT](captions.en.vtt), [English SRT](captions.en.srt), [narration transcript](narration.txt), [poster](poster.jpg), [QA](QA.json) and [provenance](PROVENANCE.json). Canonical playback is the self-hosted film in the judges-page release build. A new YouTube publication is pending the owner's account action; no current-v4 YouTube URL is claimed.

The canonical publication file uses clean picture and audio with a default English VTT track in the website player. It has no burned dialogue captions or embedded subtitle stream, so native captions do not duplicate another caption layer. The larger clean review master and burned-caption review backup remain outside this repository. The Pages derivative must remain below the 25 MiB per-file limit; its exact byte count and SHA-256 are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Actual mobile, tablet and desktop browser captures show three repair directions, the synthetic 350→354.1 km additional-distance experiment, a promoter brief, explicit acceptance of an authored AI replay, and a separate Tour SOS sample. Recorded real Qloo evidence is a distinct 350 km example: Nuremberg with taste evidence, needs-evidence without taste, with the control and both comparison omissions retaining Nuremberg. Cultural fit is not a ticket-sales forecast.

The SOS form, supplier cards, quote and simulated approval use illustrative Ho Chi Minh City suppliers and prices. No supplier is contacted, no availability is confirmed, and nothing is ordered, booked or paid. Agency subscriptions and fees on completed local services are a business hypothesis that first requires onboarding a vetted local partner network.

Touring, gear and stage scenes are labelled AI-generated illustrations, not evidence of a rescued show or fulfilled local service. Device frames, cuts, zooms, retimed tap cues and rigid scrolling of actual capture pixels are edited presentation motion. They do not establish native app animation or a physical-device installation. The narration and licensed music source are described in provenance; source recordings, full music WAVs and generation clips are not redistributed in this package.

The poster is the existing clean picture frame at 4.000 seconds, showing the illustrated tour manager. `manifest.json` records the packaged assets and hashes; the build verifies playback assets before copying them to ignored `dist/video/`. Video remains outside the PWA shell cache. The [final-v2 package](../final-v2/README.md) remains intact as an archive and rollback source.

This package describes the film and its technical validation. Account publication, third-party rights, Devpost status and deployment receipts are maintained separately by the project owner; packaging itself does not perform those actions.
